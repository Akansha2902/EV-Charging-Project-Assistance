# EV Charging Station Expansion Intelligence Dashboard ⚡

## 📊 Project Overview

An interactive **Power BI business intelligence dashboard** designed to analyze EV charging station performance, customer behavior, profitability, competitor pressure, and city-level expansion opportunities.

The project helps management identify **high-demand cities, profitable stations, underutilized locations, cost pressures, and potential areas for network expansion**.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

* Analyze EV charging demand and station utilization
* Identify high-performing and underperforming stations
* Evaluate revenue, operating costs, profit, and profit margin
* Understand customer and vehicle usage patterns
* Analyze competitor presence, pricing, and capacity
* Rank cities based on expansion opportunity
* Support data-driven expansion recommendations

## 🗂️ Dataset

The project uses multiple business tables:

* **City_Demand** – EV registrations, demand, traffic, income, and commercial activity
* **Station_Master** – station locations, chargers, charger types, status, and ratings
* **Customer_Master** – customer demographics, subscriptions, vehicles, and payment preferences
* **Charging_Sessions** – charging transactions, energy, duration, revenue, payments, ratings, and complaints
* **Station_Costs** – rent, electricity, maintenance, staffing, network, insurance, and marketing costs
* **Competitor_Stations** – competitor locations, chargers, pricing, ratings, and availability
* **Tariff_Plans** – charging prices, idle fees, minimum bills, surcharges, and discounts

## 💡 Business Value

The dashboard supports decisions such as:

* **Where should new charging stations be opened?**
* **Which stations need additional chargers?**
* **Which locations require cost optimization?**
* **Which cities have high demand but limited charging capacity?**
* **How does competitor pressure affect expansion?**

The expansion score combines demand, capacity gap, commercial strength, affordability, operational feasibility, and competition risk to support location prioritization.
